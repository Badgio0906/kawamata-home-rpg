# RPG pixel-art revision

The game now uses a coherent handheld RPG presentation: overhead town scenery,
48 × 64 player sprites, 64 × 64 food monsters, pixel panel borders and Japanese
DotGothic16 text. All sprites depict original characters and scenery.

## Saved assets and reproduction

- `assets/rpg/town_atlas.png`: original environment atlas made with the built-in
  image generation tool (not the API/CLI fallback).
- `data/town_art.json`: polygon silhouettes used by Godot to draw the atlas
  sprites without their backdrop. The source image is preserved unmodified.
- `assets/generated/player_*.png` and the food PNGs: original pixel-grid drawings
  generated by `tools/generate_rpg_sprites.py` with Pillow.
- `tools/generate_assets.py` runs the RPG sprite generator after generating the
  other assets, so rebuilding preserves the new style.
- `assets/fonts/DotGothic16.ttf`: SIL Open Font License, distributed with
  `DotGothic16-OFL.txt`. Source:
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dotgothic16

## Image generation prompt

Create production-ready original pixel art game environment asset atlas,
transparent background, 1536x1024 landscape. Exact 4 columns by 2 rows equal
cells of 384x512 pixels, generous transparent margin around each item, no grid
lines, no labels, no text. Row 1 left to right: a modern small office with slate
blue roof, a charming strawberry cake bakery with pink tiled roof and cake
emblem, a Japanese ramen diner with red roof and bowl emblem, a mint-green
convenience store with striped awning. Row 2 left to right: a warm cream Japanese
suburban home with terracotta roof, a small lavender-roof residential house, a
lush round leafy tree with visible short trunk, a wooden park bench with small
flower planter. All buildings complete, same scale, about 260px wide and 310px
tall in each cell, distinct dimensional rooftops visible from ABOVE in classic
3/4 top-down RPG orthographic view (not isometric, not frontal side-scroller).
Style: exquisite authentic Game Boy Advance-era 16-bit monster-adventure RPG
pixel art, tightly clustered square pixels, strong 1-pixel outlines at underlying
96px tile resolution, 4-tone cel shading, limited warm colorful palette, leafy
greens, detailed roof tiles, window reflections and masonry. Crisp stepped
pixels, no smooth vector shapes, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no realistic texture.
Cute cozy Japanese neighborhood at late afternoon. No people, no Pokemon
creatures, no existing game assets, no logos, no writing. Each asset clearly
separate, transparent alpha outside the individual sprite.

## Final image edit prompt

EDIT TARGET: this game sprite atlas. Remove ALL blurry background outside the 8
individual sprites and replace it with genuinely transparent alpha. Keep all 8
buildings/tree/bench precisely intact, same positions, same dimensions 1536x1024
and 4 columns 2 rows. No shadow glow or backdrop outside sprites. Preserve pixel
edges. This must be usable as a transparent PNG sprite atlas.

The returned image retained its backdrop. Godot therefore uses the explicit
polygon boundaries in `town_art.json` to display individual buildings and props.
